Use a new ID name so your theme installs next to the original instead of replacing it.

See how the home screen will look. Move and resize anything, add or remove app slots, change icon and label sizes, and make panels transparent. Click Save design before building.

Car Launcher

The home screen app that shows your themes on the car. Install it once, then install any theme you export.

  1. Install CarLauncher.apk and your exported theme APK on the head unit (USB stick or ADB).
  2. Press Home, choose Car Launcher, then Always.
  3. Long-press an empty area, then Theme, and pick your theme.
